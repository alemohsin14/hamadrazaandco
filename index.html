About the firm

A site-first contractor, not a head-office one.

Public-sector building works

Hamadraza & Co (Pvt.) Ltd. was registered in 1998 to execute civil works for provincial and federal departments. What began as a single road-maintenance contract for the C&W Department has grown into a firm running concurrent sites in four provinces, with its own plant yard, survey section, and materials laboratory.

We hold PEC Category C‑A registration, the highest classification for civil works, and are pre-qualified with NHA, WAPDA, and the Punjab C&W Department. Every project is run by a resident engineer answerable directly to the site, not routed through a regional office.

Self-performed works

Earthwork, structures, and finishing are executed with our own labour and equipment, not sub-let.

In-house lab testing

Concrete, soil, and asphalt testing done on-site against consultant specification before pour.

Resident supervision

A qualified engineer is stationed on every active site for the duration of the contract.

Own plant fleet

Excavators, rollers, batching plant, and bowsers owned and maintained, not hired per project.

Services

Scope we tender for and execute.

Each discipline below is backed by our own crews, equipment, and site testing — described here as the department scope sheets describe them.

01  ROADS

Roads & Highways

New carriageway construction, widening, and rehabilitation of national and farm-to-market roads — sub-base, base course, and asphaltic wearing course, plus road-side drainage and signage. Executed to NHA and Punjab Highways specification.

02  BUILDINGS

Public Buildings

Government office blocks, school and college buildings, basic health units, and rural health centres — from foundation to finishing, electrification, and plumbing, handed over turnkey to the client department.

03  WATER

Water Supply & Sanitation

Tubewell installation, overhead and underground reservoirs, distribution piping, and sewerage disposal schemes for PHED, TMAs, and municipal committees, including O&M handover documentation.

04  STRUCTURES

Bridges & Culverts

RCC slab and box-girder bridges, box culverts, and retaining walls along highway and canal alignments, including pile foundation work where sub-soil conditions require it.

05  EARTHWORK

Site Development

Cut-and-fill, compaction, and grading for industrial estates and housing schemes, plus internal road networks and storm-water drainage ahead of vertical construction.

06  HERITAGE

Heritage Conservation

Structural consolidation, lime-plaster restoration, and timber-work repair on protected heritage structures, executed to conservation-grade specification for the Walled City of Lahore Authority and provincial archaeology departments.
