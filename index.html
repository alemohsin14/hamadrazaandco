About Us

Building Pakistan's Infrastructure Since 1998

Company Profile

26 Years of Public-Sector Delivery

Hamadraza & Co (Pvt.) Ltd. was registered in 1998 to execute civil works for provincial and federal departments. What began as a single road-maintenance contract for the C&W Department has grown into a firm running concurrent sites in four provinces.

We hold PEC Category C-A registration, the highest classification for civil works, and are pre-qualified with NHA, WAPDA, and the Punjab C&W Department. Every project is run by a resident engineer answerable directly to the site.

Our Services

Scope We Tender For And Execute

Each discipline below is backed by our own crews, equipment, and site testing — described here as the department scope sheets describe them.

01 · ROADS

Roads & Highways

New carriageway construction, widening, and rehabilitation of national and farm-to-market roads — sub-base, base course, and asphaltic wearing course, plus road-side drainage and signage.

02 · BUILDINGS

Public Buildings

Government office blocks, school and college buildings, basic health units, and rural health centres — from foundation to finishing, handed over turnkey to the client department.

03 · WATER

Water Supply & Sanitation

Tubewell installation, overhead and underground reservoirs, distribution piping, and sewerage disposal schemes for PHED, TMAs, and municipal committees.

04 · STRUCTURES

Bridges & Culverts

RCC slab and box-girder bridges, box culverts, and retaining walls along highway and canal alignments, including pile foundation work where required.

05 · EARTHWORK

Site Development

Cut-and-fill, compaction, and grading for industrial estates and housing schemes, plus internal road networks and storm-water drainage.

06 · HERITAGE

Heritage Conservation

Structural consolidation, lime-plaster restoration, and timber-work repair on protected heritage structures, executed to conservation-grade specification.
